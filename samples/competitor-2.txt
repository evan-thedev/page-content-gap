How to Set Up a Standing Desk That You Will Actually Use

Most people buy a standing desk, stand for a week, and go back to sitting. The fix is not willpower. It is a setup that makes standing comfortable: the right height, a soft floor, neat cables, and a routine you can keep.

Getting the Height Right

Set the desk surface at elbow height when you stand. Your forearms should rest level with the keyboard and your wrists should stay neutral. Raise your monitor so the top of the screen sits at eye level, about an arm's length away. Save the sitting and standing positions on the frame's memory keypad so switching takes one press.

Cable Management

Cable management is the step everyone skips. A frame that travels up and down pulls on every cord attached to it. Mount a power strip under the desk, run one cord to the wall, and leave slack for the full range of motion. Zip ties and a simple tray keep the rest out of sight, and five minutes of cable management saves you from a snagged monitor later.

Anti Fatigue Mat

An anti fatigue mat makes long standing sessions far easier on your feet and lower back. Look for one at least three quarters of an inch thick with a beveled edge so you do not trip when you step off. Some people prefer an anti fatigue mat with raised bumps that encourage movement; others want a flat surface. Either way, it is the cheapest upgrade with the biggest effect.

Build a Routine

Start with twenty minutes of standing every hour and increase slowly. Pair standing with tasks that need less focus, such as reading email or taking calls. Change position often and take a short break to walk. The goal is to move, not to stand all day.

Desk Surface and Space

Measure your room before you order. A desk surface of 60 inches by 30 inches fits two monitors comfortably, and leave space behind the desk so the frame can rise without hitting the wall. Bamboo and laminate are both fine; laminate resists scratches and costs less.

Final Thoughts

A standing desk works when the setup does the work for you. Get the height correct, soften the floor, tidy the cables, and keep the routine. Your posture and your energy through the day will improve.
