## 1. Not a ranking guarantee

Page Content Gap compares the text of your page with the text of competitor pages you paste in.
It reports phrases, headings, and named terms that appear in their pages and not in yours.

That is all it does. It does not know how Google or any search engine ranks pages. Search
ranking is probabilistic and depends on many factors this tool never sees — links, technical
health, intent, freshness, history, and things nobody outside the search engine can measure.
Covering the gaps this tool shows may help, may do nothing, or may make a page worse if done
mechanically. Use the output as a checklist for your own judgement, not as instructions.

We make no promise, express or implied, about rankings, traffic, conversions, or revenue.

## 2. What the numbers mean

- **Gap phrase** means: the phrase meets a minimum count across the competitor pages you pasted
  and appears zero times in your page. It does not mean the phrase is important, searched for, or
  appropriate for your page.
- **Coverage %** is the share of competitor phrases or headings your page also contains, under
  the tool's counting rules. It is a description of two texts, not a quality score.
- **Entities** are found by simple pattern rules (capitalised words and a small curated list),
  not by a language model. Expect misses and false positives.
- **Suggested H2s** are competitor headings you don't have, reformatted. They are not written for
  you and may not suit your page.
- The tool has **no search volume, keyword difficulty, CPC, SERP, backlink, or crawl data** and
  does not claim to.

## 3. Privacy: everything runs in your browser

- All analysis happens in your browser using JavaScript. The text you paste is **never sent to
  our servers**, because there are none. After the page loads, the site makes no network
  requests until you click a payment link, which opens Stripe.
- Saved scenarios (if you use them) are stored in your browser's local storage on your device.
  We cannot see them, recover them, or delete them for you.
- We do not use analytics or tracking scripts in v1. If that changes, this page will say so
  first.
- Payment is handled entirely by Stripe under Stripe's privacy policy. We receive the email
  address and receipt details Stripe provides so we can support refunds and unlock recovery.

## 4. No scraping, no third-party data

third-party SEO database. Everything it analyses is text **you** paste in. You are responsible
for having the right to copy and analyse that text and for complying with the terms of the sites
it came from. Publicly visible page text pasted for private comparative analysis is, in our
view, ordinary competitive research, but that is your call and your responsibility, not ours.

Do not paste personal data you are not entitled to process. The tool is not designed for it and
we cannot help you with it.
