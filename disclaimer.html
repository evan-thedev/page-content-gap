Disclaimer, Privacy, and Terms

Plain language on purpose. Last updated: 2026-09-07.

In one sentence: Page Content Gap compares page text you paste and reports coverage gaps; it runs in your browser, uploads nothing, uses no search-volume or crawl data, and is not a ranking guarantee.

1. Not a ranking guarantee

Page Content Gap compares the text of your page with the text of competitor pages you paste in. It reports phrases, headings, and named terms that appear in their pages and not in yours.

That is all it does. It does not know how Google or any search engine ranks pages. Search ranking is probabilistic and depends on many factors this tool never sees — links, technical health, intent, freshness, history, and things nobody outside the search engine can measure. Covering the gaps this tool shows may help, may do nothing, or may make a page worse if done mechanically. Use the output as a checklist for your own judgement, not as instructions.

We make no promise, express or implied, about rankings, traffic, conversions, or revenue.

2. What the numbers mean

  • Gap phrase means: the phrase meets a minimum count across the competitor pages you pasted and appears zero times in your page. It does not mean the phrase is important, searched for, or appropriate for your page.
  • Coverage % is the share of competitor phrases or headings your page also contains, under the tool's counting rules. It is a description of two texts, not a quality score.
  • Entities are found by simple pattern rules (capitalised words and a small curated list), not by a language model. Expect misses and false positives.
  • Suggested H2s are competitor headings you don't have, reformatted. They are not written for you and may not suit your page.
  • The tool has no search volume, keyword difficulty, CPC, SERP, backlink, or crawl data and does not claim to.
